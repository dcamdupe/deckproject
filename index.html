Deck Joist Layout

Deck build · Section 1

Joist layout

Top view and side view

Layout rules follow Bunnings community guidance: joists at 450 mm centres or closer, and a 90 x 45 mm joist spanning about 1,000 mm between supports. These are not AS 1684 span tables. The bearer size of 140 x 45 mm on edge is an assumption, taken from a community example. The post position is measured to its nearest faces, and its height is not set yet. Confirm sizes, spans and footings with your certifier or engineer. Deck posts and footings are not drawn yet.
