Deck Joist Layout

Deck build · Sections 1 and 2

Joist layout

Top view and side view

Layout rules follow Bunnings community guidance: joists at 450 mm centres or closer, and a 90 x 45 mm joist spanning about 1,000 mm between supports. These are not AS 1684 span tables. The bearer size of 140 x 45 mm on edge is an assumption, taken from a community example. The structural post position is measured to its nearest faces, and its height is not set yet. The deck posts are an assumed 90 x 90 mm, three under each bearer, shown dashed in the top view because they sit below the frame. Distances between deck posts are centre to centre. Floorboards run the length of the deck, across the joists, and are drawn see-through so the frame shows below them; the last board on the right-hand side is cut down to fit. Their size, spacing and height are not confirmed, and footings are not drawn yet. Section 2 is a 200 mm step down from section 1, with its own bearers, joists and posts. Each section ends in an edge board laid across the step, at 90 degrees to the other boards, with boards on the face of the step below it. The face at the end of section 2 is assumed to be 200 mm high. The edge boards run parallel to the joists, so they and the ends of the lengthwise boards need a joist or blocking under them; that support is not drawn yet. Confirm sizes, spans and footings with your certifier or engineer.
